# Business examples

Optional documentation: [Chinese examples](zh-CN/examples.md). These English prompts can request either video language; the conversation language does not set the output language.

## Restaurant with approved copy

> Use $shotai-business-video to make a vertical Douyin video in Chinese. Use only the Riverside Kitchen collection. Read the script exactly and keep its prices. Let me compare two natural Chinese male voices and choose light instrumental music. Aim for about 45 seconds; tell me if the complete script needs more time.

Resolve the real collection. Match dishes, preparation/service actions and surroundings separately. Report missing cooking footage; a plated dish does not prove how it was prepared.

## Guesthouse with separate Chinese and English versions

> Use my approved Chinese and English scripts to make one video from each. The Chinese version is for Douyin and the English version is for TikTok. Use only this property's collection. Keep my selected Serena voice slightly faster for Chinese, offer two English voices, and use the licensed piano track I supplied.

Synthesize, measure and align each script independently. Keep the existing Chinese voice choice. Verify the music's scope and each property/room match; tags alone do not establish them.

## Café owner

> Use $shotai-business-video to turn my approved café script into a 9:16 TikTok video in English. Use only the Willow Café collection. Let me choose between two warm English voices. Pick quiet instrumental music licensed for an organic business post. Keep the exact script.

Resolve the real collection, verify drinks and service actions, offer short equal-text samples, then proceed after selection. Do not change prices or infer on-site baking from a pastry shot.

## Targeted revision

> The opening sounds too slow. Tighten only that first sentence, then add the piano track I selected. Keep the rest unchanged.

Inspect pauses before speeding up. Map the edited opening and preserve the body. Adding music alone uses stream-copy picture, with no new footage search.

## Related requests outside this skill

- Advertising copy without video editing: a writing task.
- Audience growth or ad strategy alone: marketing advice.
- Reposting downloaded videos from another account: outside the owner-footage workflow.
- Mathematical animation or software demonstrations: use the appropriate production workflow.
- Comparing TTS models alone: voice research, without a ShotAI footage or final-video requirement.
